🔥 Pavement paw‑safety guide
≤ 41°C / 105°FComfortable 46–52°C / 115–125°FHot — grass & shade ≥ 52°C / 125°FBurns pads in ~60s
Asphalt runs ~40–60°F (22–33°C) above air in direct sun, so ~75°F air ≈ 125°F pavement. Always confirm with the 7‑second back‑of‑hand test. Sources: AKC, The Farmer's Dog, Weave.
Guidance only — always use your own judgment. Heat bands follow canine heat‑illness research; flat‑faced (brachycephalic), senior, overweight and heavy‑coated dogs need extra caution.
